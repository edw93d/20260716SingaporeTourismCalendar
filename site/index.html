Methodology notes

Public listings of what is happening in Singapore — conferences, exhibitions, concerts, consumer festivals, and cruise arrivals — aggregated into existence and timing records, for people who plan around demand landing. Each source shows when it was last confirmed, above — the calendar is only ever as current as that. Coverage is still growing, so nothing here is yet a complete picture of Singapore demand. Facts only — names, dates, venues, as published. Days shown in Singapore time.
